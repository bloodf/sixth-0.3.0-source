## Game titles

The disciplines carry the names of the real games: Counter-Strike 2 and Dota 2 (Valve), Valorant (Riot Games), Overwatch (Blizzard Entertainment). The names are used only to say which game a squad plays. They are trademarks of their owners; this is a non-commercial open-source fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by them. Being free of charge does not by itself give a right to a trademark, so Settings keeps a switch to original titles should an owner ever ask.

Everything else is invented here: clubs, players, heroes, agents, maps and sponsor brands. No logos, artwork, sounds or code from those games are used.
