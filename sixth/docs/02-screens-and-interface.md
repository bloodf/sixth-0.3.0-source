# Screens and interface

The rule that shapes everything: **the building is the menu**. The headquarters fills the window. Panels cover the right part of it, and the camera moves to the room where that work is done.

## Map

```
Title ── New career: You (person, face, hair, clothes), Club, Crest, Shirt, Games, Start
  │
  └─ Headquarters ── click a room: edit, computers, seating, staff, people
       │             click a person: life, skill, contract, talk
       ├─ Build        (B) the house editor: select, ready-made, room, wall, doors, furniture, floor, paint, remove, land
       ├─ Inbox        decisions, reports, offers
       ├─ Squad        line-up, hero picks
       ├─ Training     week painter, intensity, outlook, growth
       ├─ Tactics      plan, familiarity, synergy, prep, map pool
       ├─ Staff        employed, hire
       ├─ House        people, who likes whom, house rules, diary
       ├─ HQ           rooms, seating, equipment, location
       ├─ Market       free agents, everyone, bids, scouting
       ├─ Leagues      table, fixtures, playoffs, World Masters, world ranking
       ├─ Club         money, sponsors, brand, games, honours
       ├─ Online       lobby, world ladder, private leagues, direct code, trust
       ├─ Incident     stops the day, shown where it happens
       └─ Match day ── Face-off ── Live match ── Result ── Replay
  Settings, Save sealed, The lights are off
```

## Art direction

Ink, bone and one signal yellow. Nothing is round and everything sits on a line. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed shouts, Sofia Sans explains, Anybody is kept for the logo. The club's colours come in as slabs cut at one angle, never as a tint over everything. People are photographed, not drawn: every portrait is the real 3D person, lit in a studio, with the face they are making right now.

## Live match

- Score, clock and phase at the top. Gold lead, payload or point progress under it.
- Five players on each side with their faces: health, K/D/A, weapon or hero, money or ultimate. Click one to follow that player.
- Orders at the bottom left, camera and speed at the bottom right.
- Cameras: **Action** holds one fight and switches when a hotter one starts. **Top** shows the whole map. **Arena** shows the hall. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, right-drag to turn.
- Keys: space pauses, 1 2 3 cameras, + and - speed.

## House editor

The building stays where it is and the panels leave. Tools and catalogue at the bottom, the price of the whole plan at the top, what you selected on the right.

- The pointer always says what a click would cost, or why it is not possible. Green fits, red does not.
- Rooms carry their name and level. A room that does not work yet says what is missing, and each missing thing is a link to the piece that fixes it.
- Walls between you and the rooms are cut away; V lowers or raises them all, T looks straight down, Q and E turn the view by a quarter.
- R turns, Delete sells, Ctrl Z and Ctrl Y step back and forth, Esc lets go, 1 to 0 pick the tools, WASD or dragging the ground moves the view.
- Build lists what will close before it does it. Discard leaves everything as it was.

## Component rules

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Yellow | Only the manager's next action, a choice made, and the sixth bar |
| Team colour | Slabs behind names, dock marker, HQ trim, shirts, rings |
| Lists | Share their lines. Boxes inside boxes are not allowed |
| Numbers | Large, condensed, on a ruled strip |
| Labels | Sentence case. Capitals are for names and titles |
| Six-bar meter | Quality of anything, 0 to 100 |
| Bars | Condition only: green, yellow under 60, red under 35 |
| Text | Body 15 px, never below 12.5 px |
| Dialogs | For one object (a player, a match, a confirmation). Work happens in panels |
| Empty states | Say what will be here and how it gets here |

Laid out for 1280 by 700 and up. It still works at tablet width, where panels cover the building; phones are not a target.
