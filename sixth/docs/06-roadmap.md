# Roadmap

## 0.1: the whole loop, playable

Career, four disciplines, three simulations, ten maps, living HQ, staff, gear, market, sponsors, events, leagues and Masters, brand editors, live 3D matches with orders and replays, synthesised audio, sealed saves, replay audit, P2P matches, ladder and private leagues.

## 0.2: the house, solid bodies, everything encrypted

House editor with land, walls, doors, paint, 79 pieces of furniture and ready-made rooms; people who walk through doors and around things; solid bodies, dashes and projectiles in all three simulations; headers, stores and backups encrypted; careers that cross rule versions without epochs.

## 0.3 (this version): people

Real people instead of figures, in the building, in the matches and in every portrait. Needs, feelings, quirks, friendships, grudges, dirt, house rules, a diary. Twenty-one incidents with answers. Desks and seating. The creator, the face-off, the broadcast overlay and every screen drawn again. Furniture, buildings, maps and materials made again. A renderer that photographs the building on slow machines. AGPL.

## 0.4: feel and balance

- Hero balance pass; attack-side bias; MOBA sensitivity to skill
- Draft and ban phase with counter-picks; map veto for series
- Opponent reports that change the plan, not only the prep number
- Post-match: heat maps, economy graph, key moments with jump-to
- Controller for slow machines: dynamic crowd and prop density
- Real-GPU profiling on common cards

## 0.5: depth

- Academy squads, scrim booking against named clubs, transfer windows
- Languages; captains and friendships with an effect in single rounds; partners and families
- Rivalries, press conferences, social feed
- Upper floors and stairs, a roof in photo mode, diagonal walls, saving a room as your own template, visiting a friend's house
- More maps and casts; patches that shift the meta each season
- Localisation (Portuguese, Spanish first)

## 0.6: online

- Reconnect into a duel, spectate, cups, double round robins
- Portable signed season summaries, so ladder history survives pruning
- Optional self-hosted always-on peer for leagues that want one
